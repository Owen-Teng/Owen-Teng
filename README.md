### Hi, I'm Owen

I bring a background in human resources, organizational learning, and product development to a question I care about: **how can AI help people learn, make decisions, and work together?**

My work connects management practice with hands-on product building. I'm especially interested in the moments where people and AI need to understand each other: setting a goal, sharing context, reviewing progress, and deciding what happens next.

### What I'm exploring

| Focus | Questions that guide my work |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AI & organizational learning** | How can everyday work become knowledge that a team can reuse? |
| **Human–AI collaboration** | How do we make an agent's work understandable, with room for human judgment? |
| **Gamification & interaction** | How can spaces, roles, and thoughtful feedback make complex tools more approachable? |

### What I'm building with others

**[Robinson AI Lab · 鲁滨逊](https://github.com/Robinson-AI-Lab)**  
A community I co-founded to connect AI research, management practice, and open collaboration.

**[FridayOS](https://github.com/Robinson-AI-Lab/FridayOS1.0)**  
Our exploration of a human-centered Agent OS for work: a place to organize knowledge and collaborate with AI agents. I contribute to product direction, user experience, and front-end exploration. The public 1.0 repository documents an earlier stage; the broader Agent OS vision is still being developed and tested.

### How I approach the work

- **Start with a real problem.** Let the work shape the tool.
- **Keep people involved.** Make progress visible and decisions open to review.
- **Learn by building.** Turn ideas into small experiments, then examine what actually happened.

I'm developing my front-end skills through real projects, with a focus on interaction, clarity, and usability.
